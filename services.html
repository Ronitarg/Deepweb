Vaccines & injections at home — for every age

The doctor comes to your home when a childhood vaccine or a recommended injection is needed. Care for kids, adults, and elderly patients — for Pune families.

01

Childhood vaccines at home

Immunisation visits for eligible infants, toddlers, and older children — including catch-up schedules where appropriate. Parents stay present; we explain each step in simple language and share clear aftercare for the hours that follow.

02

Recommended injections — any age

Existing doctor-recommended or prescribed injectable medicines administered at home for children, adults, and elderly patients. Bring a valid prescription and the medicine as advised.

03

Adults & elderly at home

Home injection support for working adults, post-procedure patients, and elderly or limited-mobility patients who need injectable therapy without leaving home.

04

Support for needle anxiety

Extra time and a gentle approach for children — or adults — who find injections stressful. We go at a calm pace and keep a family member close when helpful.

05

Infants & early childhood

Visits tailored for babies and toddlers — quieter surroundings where possible, and practical tips for parents after vaccines or injections.

06

Guidance & follow-up

Clear aftercare advice for parents or caregivers, warning signs to watch for, and help planning the next dose or visit.
