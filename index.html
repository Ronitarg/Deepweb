Hand hygiene & how infection spreads

A brief clinical walkthrough — proper handwashing technique, and how pathogens move from contaminated hands into the body.

01
  1. 01
    WetRinse hands thoroughly with clean running water
  2. 02
    Apply soapCreate a full lather covering both hands
  3. 03
    ScrubPalms, dorsum, between fingers, and under nails — about 20 seconds
  4. 04
    RinseWash soap and residue away under running water
  5. 05
    DryUse a clean towel or allow hands to air-dry

01Contaminated surfaces transfer microbes onto the hands during daily contact.

02Hands then touch the face — creating an entry route via mouth or mucosa.

03Once inside, pathogens may reach the gastrointestinal or respiratory tract.

04Consistent hand hygiene interrupts transmission before colonisation begins.
